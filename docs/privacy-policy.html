Mem-Mem Privacy Policy

Effective for the free source edition dated October 27, 2026. Earlier store versions may use an earlier policy until updated.

Mem-Mem is provided by SERPICO ENTERPRISES, L.L.C., publishing as Plan 9 from Outer Space. Questions: vincent.serpico@serpico.ai.

The free edition stores boards, scores, collected characters, earned choices, and preferences locally. It requires no account and includes no advertising, analytics, payment processing, or purchase-verification service. Its Android export does not request internet or network-state permission. All themes and one Quick Peek per board work offline.

Local saves remain until changed by play or removed with Android’s app-storage controls. This export disables app-data backup; manually copied files or operating-system behavior outside the app are under your control.

If you email support, we receive the address, message, and information you choose to send, and use it to respond. Email providers handle those communications under their own policies. Do not email passwords, payment information, or purchase tokens.

Google Play separately processes store downloads and any historical transactions under its policies. The free edition neither submits old purchase records nor requires them to unlock features.

Policy revisions will be published here with an updated effective date.
