## Full description

Take a little time for yourself with Mem-Mem, a matching game for adults. Flip cards, find matching pairs, and enjoy each character's celebration as you clear the board.

PLAY AT YOUR OWN PACE
Boards range from 4 to 16 cards, with smaller boards mixed in between larger ones. There is no timer or life limit. Your progress saves automatically, so you can take a break and pick up where you left off.

BUILD YOUR COLLECTION
Finish a board with 100% accuracy to earn a character choice. Browse the characters available in any theme, choose a favorite, and collect it. Discover 56 characters across seven themes, including people, firefighters, aliens, animals, snacks, and mythical creatures. All seven themes are included free.

TAKE A QUICK PEEK
Want another look? Every board includes one free, offline Quick Peek. The peek briefly reveals the remaining cards without affecting your accuracy or eligibility to earn a character choice.

MAKE IT YOURS
Choose light or dark mode and different backgrounds. Adjust sound and motion, and select any theme for your next board.

EVERYTHING INCLUDED
All seven themes, all 56 characters to earn, and one Quick Peek per board. No ads, purchases, subscriptions, account, or internet connection. Progress and your collection stay on your device.

Mem-Mem is intended for adults aged 18 and over.
