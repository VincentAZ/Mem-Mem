## Full description

Take a little time for yourself with Mem-Mem, a matching game for adults. Flip cards, find matching pairs, and enjoy each character's celebration as you clear the board.

PLAY AT YOUR OWN PACE
Boards range from 4 to 16 cards, with smaller boards mixed in between larger ones. There is no timer or life limit. Your progress saves automatically, so you can take a break and pick up where you left off.

BUILD YOUR COLLECTION
Finish a board with 100% accuracy to earn a character choice. Browse the characters available in your unlocked themes, choose a favorite, and collect it. Discover 56 characters across seven themes, including people, firefighters, aliens, animals, snacks, and mythical creatures. Four themes are included free; three more are part of Mem-Mem Plus.

TAKE A QUICK PEEK
Want another look? Free players can choose to watch a rewarded ad for one Quick Peek per board. The peek briefly reveals the remaining cards without affecting your accuracy or eligibility to earn a character choice.

MAKE IT YOURS
Choose light or dark mode and different backgrounds. Adjust sound and motion, and select an unlocked theme for your next board.

MEM-MEM PLUS
An optional one-time purchase adds Woodland Crew, Snack Squad, and Mythical Misfits: 24 more characters to earn and collect. Plus also includes one Quick Peek per board and removes all ads. Characters are earned through play; purchasing Plus does not automatically collect them.

No Mem-Mem account is required. Game progress and your collection are stored on your device. An internet connection is needed for rewarded ads, purchases, and purchase verification. Plus access can work offline for up to seven days after successful verification.

Mem-Mem is intended for adults aged 18 and over.
